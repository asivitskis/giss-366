Part 4: Results & Interpretation#

Due: Week 14 · Points: 40 · Format: Draft report, submitted to Canvas

Your app is functionally complete. Now you turn what it shows into a written result, and then into a meaning. Keep those two moves separate — that’s the whole structure of this part.

Step 1. Create charts of your results#

Your webmap alone isn’t enough to communicate your findings; export your data or results and build a minimum of two graphs/charts. Use whatever tool makes sense — Excel, an AGOL Dashboard, Python/pandas, Observable, or a JS charting library already in your stack (Chart.js, D3, Plotly).

Under each chart, write a short description of what it shows. This text can move directly into your Results section below.

Step 2. Draft your Analysis & Results section (~0.5–1 page)#

State your outputs. No interpretation yet — that’s Step 3. Describe, in words, the patterns your final webmap and analysis actually show. Report quantitative results too (attribute table values, summary statistics, your Step 1 charts). Number and caption every figure/table you reference, including:

  • A link to and screengrab of your final webmap/app

  • The charts from Step 1

  • Any other output tables

Step 3. Draft your Discussion & Conclusions section (~0.5–1 page)#

Now interpret. Move beyond the numbers and describe what your results mean relative to the problem you posed in your Introduction:

  • What’s the big-picture significance? How does this connect to the larger problem or current thinking on it?

  • Why do these results matter — to your named audience, and to anyone who might fund or scale this work?

Step 4. Confirm your deployed map is near-complete#

Do a final pass on your live webmap/app: broken links, missing popups, unstyled layers, anything left over from earlier drafts. Part 5a’s peer review will be looking at this directly.

Submitting Part 4#

  • Your two (or more) charts with descriptions (Step 1)

  • Analysis & Results draft (Step 2)

  • Discussion & Conclusions draft (Step 3)

  • Confirmation your webmap/app link is live and current (Step 4)
