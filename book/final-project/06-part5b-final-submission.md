# Part 5b: Final Submission

**Due:** Week 16 · **Points:** 90 · **Format:** Full package — deployed webmap/app, technical report, portfolio synthesis

This is the final package: your deployed product, the written report that documents it, and a short reflection connecting this project back to the semester's labs. Nothing here asks you to redo prior work — only to revise it using your Part 4 draft and Part 5a peer/class feedback.

## 1. Deployed webmap/app (confirm and finalize)

Do a last pass on your live product:

- Confirm the link is public and loads without you being logged in
- Fix anything flagged in peer review or your own Part 5a demo run
- Confirm sharing/access settings match the audience you named in Part 1 — and that you can explain, in the report, what those settings cost or grant that audience

## 2. Technical report

Revise your Part 4 drafts using peer and class feedback. Single-spaced, size 11 font minimum, figures numbered and captioned, MLA-style references. Full section guidance:

**Introduction** (≤5 sentences) — purpose/problem, study area and why it was chosen, significance and audience (refer to Part 1).

**Background Information** (≤5 sentences) — general context, the assumptions/simplifications your timeline and resources required, optional background figures (refer to Part 2, Step 5).

**Data & Methods** (~0.5–1 page) — what data you used and how you collected it (refer to Part 1's plan and Part 2's data check); your architecture and stack justification (refer to Part 2, Step 1); figures welcome (a field photo, your collection flowchart).

**Analysis & Results** (~0.5–1 page) — your finalized version of the Part 4 draft: outputs only, descriptive, quantitative results included, figures/tables numbered and captioned.

**Discussion & Conclusions** (~0.5–1 page) — your finalized interpretation from Part 4: what your results mean for the problem, why they matter, how this could scale.

**Future Work** (≤5 sentences) — what you'd do with more time, money, or data; what additional analysis you'd run.

**References** — MLA format; include full metadata for any external data source you incorporated (creator, date, where acquired). See the [USC Library MLA guide](http://libguides.usc.edu/c.php?g=234918&p=1559112) for examples.

## 3. Portfolio synthesis

A short (~1 page) reflection connecting this project to your semester of lab work. Address:

- Which 2–3 labs most directly fed into this project's architecture, data collection, or analysis, and how?
- Revisit your three ethics questions one last time, specifically for the product you shipped: Who is your audience, and who isn't it reaching? What did your platform/service choice cost that audience? Who controls this data now, and who agreed to that?
- If you were advising next semester's version of yourself, what would you tell them to plan for earlier?
